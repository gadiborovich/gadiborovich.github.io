Follow-on ownership math

A simple comparison of what a $75K follow-on buys under three potential Series A valuations and two discount scenarios.

$10K
At a $30M valuation
Intended investment
$100K

Follow-on available
$75K
For the current investment

Starting point

The original $10K investment at $30M bought approximately 0.0333%. The $100K investment we wanted to make at that price would have bought approximately 0.3333%.

We bought 10% of the position we originally wanted.

75% discount

A 75% discount means investing at 25% of the Series A price. The new $75K receives the same number of shares as $300K invested at the Series A price.

Series A valuation Effective conversion price Ownership from new $75K Headline total* % of original target
$150M $37.5M 0.2000% 0.2333% 70%
$200M $50M 0.1500% 0.1833% 55%
$300M $75M 0.1000% 0.1333% 40%

50% discount

A 50% discount means investing at 50% of the Series A price. The new $75K receives the same number of shares as $150K invested at the Series A price.

Series A valuation Effective conversion price Ownership from new $75K Headline total* % of original target
$150M $75M 0.1000% 0.1333% 40%
$200M $100M 0.0750% 0.1083% 32.5%
$300M $150M 0.0500% 0.0833% 25%

* Headline totals add the original 0.0333% without accounting for dilution. Exact ownership depends on the current fully diluted cap table and the final SAFE and Series A mechanics.
