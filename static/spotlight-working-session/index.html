A brief learning on price and judgment

We started wanting to meet founders before YC and invest at lower prices. We underestimated how competitive the environment would become. We still want the right entrepreneurs at the best available price, while judging the opportunities actually available to us.

Place Labs / Intelligent Machines

With Place Labs, we got both the team and early entry: $150k on a $6m post-money SAFE. Intelligent Machines chose to wait until its seed. We had worked with the team and introduced the first customer; we still chose to invest after the round became much more competitive and expensive.
