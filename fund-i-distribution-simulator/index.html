How this works & sources

The normal chart window is 2024–2034. Fund I fundraising began in 2022 and the final close was in December 2024; the chart’s starting year does not reset anyone’s investment date. Prepared cash years reflect companies already underway and remain hypothetical. Manually entered later receipts or payment delays extend the chart; 2034 is a viewing horizon, not a stated legal fund term.

Company equity value × modeled ownership × percentage sold gives hypothetical fund proceeds, with any selected haircut. Company inputs are independent; one sale per company is modeled. Financing marks and possible acquirers are starting points for a discussion, not available offers or promised exits.

The fund reports $140,000 distributed against $2,087,500 contributed including fees. Shopgenie is history, not a second future sale. Its retained/recycled proceeds are not added again. Historical amounts are source-reported; no new bank-settlement audit is implied.

Ownership bases vary. Dated pro formas include their modeled rounds; entry estimates require dilution from entry. Security preferences, taxes and individual LP terms are outside this simplified illustration. The cumulative LP chart applies additional reserves, retention, a common carry assumption, and an LP payment delay. It starts with 20% carry above capital returned, no additional reserves or retention, and same-year payment. Historical payments use the official October 15, 2025 distribution date; individual bank settlements varied. It is not the administrator’s waterfall. No future capital calls or returns on reinvested proceeds are assumed.

Every active company has editable hypothetical values, dilution and cash years. These are discussion assumptions, not expected outcomes. Unknown ownership remains a required input. Switching full exit / partial sale changes only the fraction of our position sold. Exit references offer perspective on possible buyers and outcomes. Some are reported discussions or adjacent transactions; their headline values are not directly interchangeable. Sources and dates are available inside each company’s context.
